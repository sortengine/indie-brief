Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Wednesday 30 September.

Modern Times Review reports on Inconvenient Films, the Baltic region's only human rights documentary festival, which is marking twenty years of operation. In an interview, festival founder Gediminas Andriukaitis discusses building a programme around impact and the philosophy behind the festival's approach to programming, noting that bringing together people who already agree amounts to talking to a mirror. For documentary makers focused on human rights subjects, Inconvenient Films remains a distinctive outlet in the Baltic region.

Deadline reports that Final Payment, directed by Chinese filmmaker Niu Niu, has won the Best Film Award in the Fei Mu Awards at this year's Pingyao International Film Festival. The film also took Best Actor for Niu Jianrong's performance. Meanwhile, Congo Boy, directed by Rafiki Fariala, won Best Film in the festival's Roberto Rossellini Awards. Both awards represent significant recognition for independent and international cinema at one of Asia's major film festivals.
