Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Wednesday 21 October.

Deadline reports on documentary momentum heading into awards season. Four Oscar-contending features are gaining traction with critics and industry arbiters: American Doctor, To Hold a Mountain, Closure, and Once Upon a Time in Harlem. Filmmakers behind these distinguished productions recently assembled at the Hot Springs Documentary Film Festival to discuss their work as the awards race begins to take shape.

In theatrical release news, Deadline has exclusive details on The Cycle of Love, an Oscar-winning romantic documentary from director Orlando von Einsiedel and executive produced by Priyanka Chopra Jonas. Fresh from earning two nominations for the Critics Choice Documentary Awards, the film will return to cinemas for a special one-day-only engagement on November 1, positioned as a date night theatrical event.

Screen Daily reports that the Sundance Institute has named Tracy Brady as head of marketing, brand, and communications. Brady will be based in Boulder, Colorado, reporting to Sundance CEO David Linde, bringing executive leadership to the prestigious institution's communications strategy.

Also from Screen Daily, LuckyChap International has appointed a top UK executive to lead its film slate. The longtime Searchlight UK executive has overseen films including Martin McDonagh's awards season hopeful Wild Horse Nine, bringing significant production experience to the company's upcoming projects.

Finally, POV Magazine highlights documentary programming across the autumn festival circuit, with notable selections at Planet in Focus Environmental Film Festival, ReelWorld Film Festival, and Rendezvous with Madness.
