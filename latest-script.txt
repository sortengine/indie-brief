Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Friday 13 November.

Realscreen reports that Po Si Teng's American Doctor and Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim's You Can See Everything are leading the nominations for the 20th Cinema Eye Honors, with seven nominations each including top documentary. The Cinema Eye Honors celebrate excellence in non-fiction filmmaking.

In acquisition news, Realscreen reports that Munich-based factual content company Autentic has acquired the assets of French factual producer and distributor Gedeon Media Group, including Gedeon Programmes, Docland Yard and MC4. The deal strengthens Autentic's position in European factual production and distribution.

Realscreen also reports that Netflix has greenlit a feature documentary on Russell Brand from production company 72 Films, set for quick-turnaround release.

Looking ahead to festivals and markets, Modern Times Review reports that the 35th World Congress of Science and Factual Producers will take place from 8 to 11 November 2027 in Riva del Garda in Trentino, developed with local research and cultural institutions. Meanwhile, the East Doc Platform in Prague has opened its call for documentary projects from Central and Eastern Europe, with free applications accepted until 13 December 2026 for the 16th edition next year.

And Screen Daily reports that European distributors are grappling with increasingly selective pay-one deals from pay-TV firms and streamers, with some saying they've never seen market conditions quite this challenging.
