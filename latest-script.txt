Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Friday 6 November.

We start in Italy, where significant policy changes are afoot. Screen Daily reports that Italy has passed a sweeping film and TV reform that includes measures to attract international shoots. The reform paves the way for a new cinema agency and a restructuring of public incentives, making it potentially attractive for co-productions and international productions looking for tax incentives and support.

Moving to co-production news, Realscreen reports that Off the Fence, part of World Storytelling Media Group, has agreed to pre-buy and coproduce the ten by sixty-minute immersive factual series Wrecks and Riches with Hearst. The series comes from Emmy-nominated producers, signalling strong commercial potential for distributors in the factual space.

On commissioning, Welsh pubcaster S4C has opened calls for digital commissions and factual YouTube documentaries, according to Realscreen. This represents a new opportunity for producers looking to develop content for digital platforms through a traditional broadcaster.

Also on the commissioning front, Realscreen reports that the CBC in Canada is launching an editorial initiative focused on personal finance and money matters. CBC Money will develop and commission a slate of cross-platform programming, offering further opportunities for factual producers in that space.

Turning to international markets, Deadline reports that Weta FX, the New Zealand-based visual effects powerhouse, is partnering with Taiwan Creative Content Agency, or TAICCA, to position Taiwan as an AI hub for Asia's content industries. The partnership involves investment into Taiwanese VFX house Moonshine Studio, which will serve as Weta's outpost in Taiwan.

And finally, Deadline reports that in a move focused on independent film, Bankside is launching world sales on the supernatural horror film In Her Shadow ahead of the AFM. The project stars Letitia Wright and comes from writer-director Rowan Athale of Gangs of London, with producers including Tea Shop Films, the team behind Obsession.
