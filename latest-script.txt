Deadline reports that the Doc Talk Podcast is hitting the road this autumn with filmmakers behind several acclaimed documentaries. The series will feature makers of Union Town, Black Sunflowers, and Edward Said: Between Worlds, alongside leaders from the Camden International Film Festival. It's a timely move following a busy festival season that saw Venice, Telluride, and Toronto back-to-back in September, with Camden providing a dedicated platform for documentary lovers.

Also in documentary development, Deadline has an exclusive on a psychological thriller short called Alone, which wrapped recently under writer-director Garrett Patten. The film features a majority deaf cast including Oscar winner Troy Kotsur, BAFTA-nominated Millicent Simmonds from A Quiet Place, Emmy nominee Nyle DiMarco, and two-time Tony winner Phylicia Rashad. Patten and co-writer Tiziano Tucci are now eyeing a feature expansion, which could offer interesting opportunities for financiers and producers interested in inclusive casting and deaf representation in genre filmmaking.
