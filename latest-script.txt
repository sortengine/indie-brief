Realscreen reports that Prime Video has set a November 30 premiere date for its three-part documentary series on Sam Bankman-Fried, the former head of FTX. The series explores one of the most significant financial scandals in recent years.

Also in documentary news, Realscreen reports that Neon has joined The Guardian and mk2 Films to expand the reach of the documentary NAZA, directed by Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor. The film has already been released independently, and this partnership will help it reach wider audiences and support an awards campaign.

In festival news, Modern Times Review reports that the 30th Ji.hlava International Documentary Film Festival opens this Friday, 23 October, in the Czech city of Jihlava, running until later in the week. The festival opens with a film on choreographer Jiří Kylián.

Screen Daily reports that Warsaw is in the running to host a possible second edition of the Lumiere Summit in 2027. The CNC and the Polish Film Institute are exploring the feasibility of bringing this important industry gathering back to Central Europe.

Also from Realscreen, the production company EverWonder Studio, backed by Jeff Zucker's RedBird IMI, has signed exclusive overall production deals with producer-directors Zeberiah Newman and Vanessa Golembewski. The deals will see the pair develop projects across the studio's slate.

And finally, Realscreen reports that Blue Planet III, the six-part natural history series narrated by Sir David Attenborough, will premiere on January 23 on BBC America and AMC Plus.
