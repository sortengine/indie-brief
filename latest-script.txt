Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Monday 5 October.

Deadline reports that Regal Entertainment producer Keith Monteverde is teaming with Singapore's Mocha Chai Laboratories on a coming-of-age drama called One For The Road. The film is directed by award-winning Filipino filmmaker Kip Oebanda and stars Elijah Canlas, Gian Bernardino, Emilio Daez and JM Ibarra as four high school best friends. It's a notable co-production bringing together Philippine and Singaporean production talent.

In documentary distribution news, Magnolia Pictures' The History of Concrete continues its strong theatrical run. The film, which premiered at Sundance, has crossed one point two million dollars at the box office and is now the top-grossing platform-released documentary of 2026. According to Deadline, it made four hundred and fifty thousand dollars across one hundred and sixty screens in its third weekend.

Screen Daily reports that the Zurich Film Festival has announced its 2026 winners, with top prizes going to French and US films. The selection underscores the continued importance of established festivals in championing independent and international cinema.

And finally, Lee Child, author of the Jack Reacher books, has explained his decision to pursue the Prime Video series adaptation over a third Tom Cruise film. Child says he exercised a veto on another Cruise movie, calling the streaming series a fabulous luxury that gives his work greater creative freedom. The show is now preparing fifth and sixth seasons.
