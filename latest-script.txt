Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Thursday 15 October.

Realscreen reports that CJ ENM has secured a Swedish adaptation of its dating format Couple Palace, which will air on TV4 as Like to Love and is produced by Banijay. It's another win for the Korean company's international format strategy.

DOC NYC has revealed its shortlists for awards contenders across features and shorts at the 17th edition of the festival, with the full lineup now announced. That's a key marker for documentary producers heading into the final stretch of the awards season.

The Critics Choice Documentary Awards have released their nominations, with Ava DuVernay's Netflix documentary 14th leading the field. The film explores the 14th Amendment to the US Constitution as a follow-up to her earlier work 13th.

Realscreen reports that Radial Entertainment and financier Content Partners have announced a revival of the true crime series Forensic Files with 20 new episodes. The companies will jointly finance the season and share production responsibilities.

The BFI and Chanel filmmaker awards have unveiled their 2026 winners, with filmmakers behind We The Hated, Lady, Ish and Something Familiar each receiving twenty thousand pounds in funding. That's significant support for emerging independent voices.

Realscreen also reports that Peacock has greenlit a new reality competition series called Assassins from Sony Pictures Television-owned B17 Entertainment, with Scarlett Johansson as executive producer.

The European Film Academy has unveiled its shortlist for the 2027 European Film Awards, including major festival winners such as Fjord, Woman Unknown, Yellow Letters, Tender Loving Care and La Bola Negra.

Finally, Realscreen reports that National Geographic has unveiled its eight-part docuseries Megastorm on the world's most devastating weather disasters from 72 Films, alongside a one-off ocean special from Offspring.
