Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Tuesday 10 November.

Realscreen reports that National Geographic is partnering with Walt Disney Animation Studios on a new natural history series called The Real Frozen, set to premiere on National Geographic. The collaboration brings together the broadcaster's documentary expertise with Disney's animation capabilities.

In commissioning news, Realscreen also reports that ITV has ordered a new health and wellness series called Eat, Cook, Move with Davina, produced by London-based Whisper and featuring former Big Brother presenter Davina McCall. The show is set for ITV1.

On the production side, Autentic has greenlit a new factual series from Toronto-based independent producer Go Button Media. The first project is Engineering Marvels, a six by fifty-two minute series exploring the ingenuity behind major infrastructure projects.

Screen Daily reports that Edinburgh prize-winner Skintown has been acquired for UK and Ireland distribution. The film stars Screen Star of Tomorrow Jack Rowan and is directed by Kieron J. Walsh.

At the markets, Screen Daily notes that UK sales agents are heading to the AFM boosted by what they're calling a return of sophistication in independent film. Despite the AFM being described as the least sexy market, sellers are looking forward to getting business done efficiently.

Finally, documentary distribution specialist Dogwoof has appointed Sam Joyce as sales and acquisitions manager, ahead of next year's International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam.
