Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Saturday 10 October.

Ava DuVernay's documentary Fourteenth has premiered as the closing night film of the New York Film Festival. Screen Daily reports the film is a rousing exploration of the current attack on the rights of Black Americans, following her Oscar-nominated earlier work Thirteenth. Fourteenth will stream on Netflix from December.

Realscreen reports significant executive moves in the industry. David Brindley, most recently CEO and executive producer at UK production company Twofour, has been appointed to the pivotal creative leadership role of content chief at Channel 4. Meanwhile, Sharon Levy is leaving her post as CEO of Banijay's Endemol Shine North America to head up Fremantle's Americas and Australia operations. And Gerhard Zeiler, president of international for Warner Bros Discovery, is departing the company following its recent merger with Paramount Skydance.

Modern Times Review highlights two major documentary festivals expanding their reach. Ji.hlava in the Czech Republic has unveiled a 350-film programme for its 30th edition, running October 23 to November 1, featuring a Trinh T Minh-ha retrospective and new public talk series, with short films streaming free online. The human rights documentary festival Verzió is showcasing six Hungarian competition films at its 23rd edition, several from first-time directors exploring how family and historical legacies shape the present.

At MIPCOM, the Asian Contents and Film Market, which is the industry platform of Busan International Film Festival, is launching new programmes including InnoAsia, which hosts panels, workshops and events on advanced technologies and innovative business strategies, including artificial intelligence, with market spotlights on Thailand and Japan.

Finally, V Studios, the Vietnamese production arm of Korean exhibitor CJ CGV, has sealed multiple international deals on its supernatural fantasy Red Woman, including sales to NewGates Corporation for Japan, Mockingbird Pictures for North America, and Infinity Entertainment for Australia.
