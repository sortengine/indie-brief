Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Friday 9 October.

The Grierson Trust has announced Anna Godas, CEO and co-founder of Dogwoof, as the recipient of the Grierson Trustees Award for 2026. The honour recognises Godas's significant contributions to documentary.

The International Documentary Association has announced the honourees for the 2026 IDA Documentary Awards, celebrating excellence across the documentary field.

Realscreen reports that Fremantle has begun the international rollout of its entertainment format Stacks of Cash, which has been commissioned by CBS in the United States and the Seven Network in Australia.

At the Vancouver International Film Festival, Ceremony, directed by Banchi Hanuse, won Best Canadian Documentary, among other awards announced for 2026.

Realscreen also reports that the Grierson Trust is honouring documentary with MIPCOM Picks covering nail-biting suspense and stranger-than-fiction true stories, while international distributors are presenting their full slates at the market this week.

Screen Daily reports that the Venice Golden Lion winner Woman Unknown has been acquired for the UK and Ireland and is screening in competition at the BFI London Film Festival, which has opened its 70th edition.
