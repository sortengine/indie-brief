Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Thursday 5 November.

Realscreen reports that Jenny Groom, a former executive at NBCUniversal and BBC Studios, has been appointed head of MGM Alternative at Amazon MGM Studios, effective November 30th. This is a significant leadership move in the unscripted production space.

In major regulatory news, the UK government has issued a Public Interest Intervention Notice regarding the planned acquisition of ITV's media and entertainment holdings by Comcast-owned Sky. UK Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy gave the go-ahead for this intervention on Wednesday, which will likely shape the future landscape of independent production in the UK.

TrustNordisk has acquired international sales rights to the Viking drama Stiklestad, which will be presented at the upcoming American Film Market. The film will be directed by Erik Skjoldbjaerg, known for Insomnia, which was later remade by Christopher Nolan.

Realscreen also reports that Toronto-based distributor Syndicado has signed an agreement with UK-headquartered TVF International for more than 300 hours of programming across all major English-speaking territories for digital, home video and educational platforms. This represents a significant distribution deal for independent content.

On the documentary front, La Bola Negra won the BFI London Film Festival 2026 audience award, while Dependence won the audience award for best British discovery, highlighting strong documentary work emerging from recent festivals.

Finally, Realscreen reports that BossaNova Media has unveiled a pilot digital partnership with Energy UK, the energy industry trade association, to produce short-form episodes spanning science, engineering and history, showing new commissioning opportunities in the educational and industry sectors.
