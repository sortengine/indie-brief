Good morning, this is Indie Brief for Friday 2 October.

Pakistan has made history at the Academy Awards by selecting a documentary as its official entry for Best International Feature. Deadline reports that Mo Naqvi's Hanging By a Wire, a nonfiction action thriller about a major news event from 2023, is the first documentary Pakistan has ever chosen for this distinction.

Realscreen reports that Screen Australia has announced more than five point seven million Australian dollars in production and development funding for 47 documentaries, supporting both established and emerging teams.

Cineflix Rights has secured presales for the three-part docuseries Senna: The Last Race, with buyers including Bell Media, SBS, and Warner Bros. Discovery Latin America. Realscreen reports the series is now in distribution through the London-based Cineflix Rights.

One Tribe TV has been commissioned by ZDF to produce a documentary on Thomas Cromwell, Henry VIII's advisor, for the German-French cultural channel Arte. Realscreen has the details.

The MIA Market is returning to Rome with more than 630 submissions and a focus on financing. Modern Times Review reports the market for film, series and animation will feature a new European Investment Hub and an expanded financing programme.

And looking ahead to festivals, the One World International Documentary Film Festival in Bratislava opens on 20 October with BONDS as its theme, according to Modern Times Review.
